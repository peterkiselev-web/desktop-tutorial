Phone repairs

Expert repairs for the phone you rely on.

From a shattered screen to a phone that keeps restarting, we find the real fault, explain it in plain English and give you a clear price before any work begins.

  • Screen replacement

    Cracked glass, black spots, lines, flickering or touch that stopped responding. Replaced and fully tested.

  • Battery replacement

    Dying by lunchtime or shutting down at 20%? A new battery brings back a full day of charge.

  • Charging port

    Loose cable, slow charging or not charging at all. Sometimes it needs a careful clean, sometimes a new port.

  • Camera & lens

    Blurry photos, a cracked lens cover or a camera that won't focus or open.

  • Back glass & housing

    Shattered back glass and damaged frames restored, so your phone looks and feels right again.

  • Water damage

    Switch it off, don't charge it and bring it straight in. The sooner we see it, the better the chances.

  • Software & faults

    Constant restarts, boot loops, failed updates and the faults you can't see from the outside.

  • Speakers & mics

    Can't hear your calls, or callers can't hear you? We trace the fault and fix it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us on 07999 910101

Which phones do you repair?

We repair iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel and most other makes, as well as iPads, Android tablets and computers.

Are your repairs guaranteed?

Yes. Every repair is backed by our guarantee. If anything isn't right with the work we've done, bring it back and we'll put it right.

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on the model and the fault. Call, text or pop in and we'll give you a clear price before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Just come in during opening hours. If you'd like to check we have the part for your model first, call us on 07999 910101.

Will I lose my data?

Most repairs, like screens, batteries and charging ports, don't touch your data. We still recommend backing up your phone before any repair, just in case.

Do you buy and sell phones?

Yes. We sell quality-checked, guaranteed iPhones and Android phones, and we buy phones too. Bring yours in with photo ID for a fair, no-obligation offer.

Where are you and when are you open?

We're at 8 Sutherland Avenue, Maida Vale, London W9 2HQ. We're open Monday to Saturday from 10:30am to 8:30pm, and Sunday from 12pm to 5pm.

What else can you help with?

Tablet and computer repairs, virus removal, CCTV and networking, smart home setup, plus phone accessories such as cases, chargers and screen protectors.
